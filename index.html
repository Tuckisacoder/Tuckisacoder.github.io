Progressive Memory Trainer

Practise each monologue through a ladder of harder styles, or fix one level and drill it. You can also run the whole scene in order and answer the cues around you.

Setup

Use NAME: line for speeches, with each verse line on its own line. [brackets] are stage directions. [MOVE NAME: action] marks a movement you perform. Any other text becomes passages to practise.

I play (choose all your roles)
Practice level

Easy hides about one word in four, medium about half, hard every word. On the ladder, every line is practiced once per loop, and levels only change when a loop ends. Then each line moves on its own result: one clean recall clears a level, two clean loops in a row jump two levels, and a clearly missed line moves back. A fixed level drills one kind of practice and does not change anyone’s progress.

Answer hidden words by

Word bank: tap a gap, then a word (or just tap words to fill gaps in order). Typing: Enter moves to the next blank, Ctrl or Cmd + Enter checks. On the ladder, a clean recall queues a level up (two clean loops in a row queue two), a bad miss queues a drop, a near miss or a peek holds. Changes apply when the loop ends.

Running the scene

The other characters' speeches and movements appear on their own. When it is your turn, type your speech from memory, or tap Done once you have made your move. The page cannot hear or see you, so movement is self-checked.
